# Landing surface brief

Scope: `/` with shared navigation into `/plan` and `/analyze`. Visitor mode: Persuade on the landing page; Operate on both task pages. Audience: Chinese education managers, teachers and parents. Job: understand course-to-exam guidance and paper-to-evidence analysis within seconds, then act. Proof: real CCF clauses, real course calculations and working interface demonstrations. Constraints: no invented claims, no official endorsement, code-led build, responsive and reduced-motion aware. The user explicitly replaced the previous visual world and requested a showy MotionSites landing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An Orbit-style evidence theater replaces the generic education SaaS landing; a dimensional paper sculpture exposes how a question becomes a CCF-backed judgment.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ink, oversized GESP serif lettering, warm paper layers, amber judgment marks and luminous green evidence paths. Dimensional documents, scan guides, particles and morphing evidence panels form the component language.

STORY: Visitors watch a paper open into its question, CCF clause and verdict, then choose course progress or upload a paper. The second viewport maps four real course stages to GESP levels and shows one existing calculation example.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A huge low-contrast GESP word sits behind a left-aligned three-line promise and two actions. On the right, layered CCF and question papers tilt in perspective; pointer or click reveals the question, clause and verdict with animated beams. Mobile stacks the same scene and reduced motion opens the complete static verdict.

FORM: Pinned “Orbit 证据剧场”, based on the confirmed-free MotionSites Orbit Flora and the user’s “开始，别再收敛”; seed: none (pinned). Signature interaction: pointer/click morph-reveal of the dimensional paper evidence stack, followed by a continuous course-to-exam path.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
